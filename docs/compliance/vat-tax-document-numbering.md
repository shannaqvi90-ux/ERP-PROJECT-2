# Numbering of tax invoices and tax credit notes

Checked: 2026-10-30. Signed off: the owner, 2026-10-30 (needs-human #32). Owning piece:
p10-numbering (sequence settings); later invoicing pieces keep rules 2 and 6.

## What the law requires

- A Tax Invoice carries "a sequential Tax Invoice number or a unique number which enables
  identification of the Tax Invoice and the order of the Tax Invoice in any sequence of invoices"
  (Executive Regulation, Article 59, Clause 1(d)). Clause 16, added for electronic invoices, does not
  lift Clause 1, so this also holds for an Electronic Invoice.
- A Tax Credit Note has no number requirement in Article 60 (Clause 1 lists items a to g; none is a
  number). An Electronic Credit Note needs "a unique identification" (mandatory fields document).
- No official publication found says anything about gaps in a sequence, restarting a sequence, or
  reusing a number (searched: the Executive Regulation, Ministerial Decisions 243 of 2025 and 197 of
  2026, the Electronic Invoicing Guidelines V 2.0, the mandatory fields document V 1.1, and the FTA's
  VAT Administrative Exceptions Guide).

## Rule (the product's, stricter than the law)

Applies to the document types `sales.invoice` (Tax Invoice) and `sales.creditNote` (Tax Credit
Note), and to every tax document type added later.

1. **Gapless, always.** The sequence is gapless and the setting cannot be turned off, through the
   screen or the API. A gapless sequential number satisfies both readings of Article 59(1)(d) and
   leaves nothing to explain to an auditor.
2. **A number is given once.** A number once given is never given again, changed or deleted. A
   cancelled document keeps its number (it is cancelled by a credit note or marked cancelled, never
   renumbered).
3. **Never backwards.** The next number may be set only before the first number of a period has
   been given; after that it follows on and cannot be moved.
4. **Reset only with the period in the number.** A yearly reset is allowed only when the pattern
   holds the year, a monthly reset only when it holds the year and the month, so every number stays
   unique within the company and its place in the order is clear. Without those tokens the
   sequence never resets.
5. **Pattern changes apply forward.** A change to the prefix, suffix or padding applies to the next
   number only. Numbers already given stay as they were. A change that could give a number already
   given is refused.
6. **One sequence per company and document type.** Each company (each registrant, with its own TRN)
   numbers its own tax invoices and its own tax credit notes, in separate sequences.
7. **Changes are on the record.** Every change to a tax document sequence's settings is kept in the
   audit trail: who changed what, and when (CLAUDE.md rule 4).

Credit notes follow the same rule although Article 60 asks for no number: one rule for both is
simpler for users, and the stricter form already meets the e-invoice "unique identification".

## Product today (2026-10-30)

p10-numbering r1 (87bb78f5) ships `sales.invoice` (`INV-{YYYY}-`) and `sales.creditNote`
(`CN-{YYYY}-`) as gapless by default, but an administrator can turn gapless off and change the
reset period freely. The p10 r1 critic also found a sequence that switches from yearly to never and
back within a year left stuck (every take refused), which rule 3 and rule 5 do not allow either.
p10 enforces rules 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 for these types in its next round, with English and Arabic
reasons on screen; rules 2 and 7 hold already (the issued numbers' unique index and the audit
trigger) and later invoicing pieces keep rule 2.
