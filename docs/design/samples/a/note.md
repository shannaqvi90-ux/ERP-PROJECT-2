# Direction A: "Fluent, faster"

Closest to Dynamics 365 in working style: a dark ink app bar, a tree navigation on the left, a one-row command bar, a page header with key facts, a dense grid with a details pane (contacts) or a factbox (form). Cleaner and quicker than the original: one accent, no grey slabs, shorter rows.

**Palette.** Brand ink #09071F as the app bar, violet #6A00E8 (light) / #9B5CFF (dark) as the single accent. Neutrals are cool and slightly violet (page #F3F3F8, surface white, lines #E0DFEA). Status colours are quiet tinted pills (green, amber, red, blue), all AA on their tints. High contrast is black with white borders, yellow focus and a light violet accent.

**Type.** English: IBM Plex Sans (variable, SIL OFL). Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400 to 700 (SIL OFL), drawn to match Plex Latin, so mixed lines (names, TRNs, emails) sit on one baseline. Scale 11/12/13/14/16/20/24 px; Arabic is one pixel larger with 1.7 line height. Tabular figures on every number and amount.

**Spacing and shape.** 4 px grid. Radius 2 to 6 px, 1 px lines, small shadows only on menus. Compact: 28 px rows and controls (default). Comfortable: 38 px rows.

**Components.** App bar with global search and a visible density switch; collapsible navigation groups with a left accent bar on the open page; command bar with icons, one filled primary and an overflow menu; sticky grid header with sort icon and resize handle; row hover, selection with an edge bar; filter chips; labels left of fields in a 2-column section grid; money field with a currency chip and the base-currency line; dates dd/mm/yyyy with a picker; inline validation (TRN check mark, licence-expiry warning); credit meter; status bar with keyboard hints.

**Why better.** Than Dynamics 365: the same muscle memory with less chrome, 28 px rows in Compact, currency beside every amount, brackets and red for negatives, real right-to-left, and no clipped buttons. Than Odoo: a persistent command bar and factbox instead of a smaller form with chatter below, a details pane that keeps the list in view, dense but aligned numbers, and a consistent 3-theme token set. Best fit for accountants and clerks who live in lists all day.
