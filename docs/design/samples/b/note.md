# Direction B: "Quiet and light"

Modern software-as-a-service feel: almost no lines, space and tint do the separating. A tinted sidebar with a jump-to search, no top bar, a large title, key facts as plain figures, a floating command bar, and a details card with soft depth.

**Palette.** White page, a barely-violet sidebar (#F7F6FC), filled fields instead of boxed ones. Ink #09071F is the secondary "on" colour (active chip, selected role card); violet #6A00E8 / #9B5CFF is reserved for the one primary action, selection and focus. Dark theme is the brand night #0E0B1A. High contrast restores visible borders through one token.

**Type.** English: Inter (variable, SIL OFL). Arabic: Noto Sans Arabic (variable, SIL OFL), already bundled by the product. Titles 30 px at weight 650 with slightly negative tracking; body 13.5 px. In Arabic, titles go to 32 px, body to 14.5 px, line height 1.7. Tabular figures throughout.

**Spacing and shape.** Airy: Comfortable is the default (48 px rows, 40 px controls); Compact (34 px rows) is one click away. Radius 6 to 22 px. Layered, low-opacity shadows; rows have no separators, only a rounded hover and selection; sticky header is translucent.

**Components.** Sidebar with brand tile, app icon and both wordmarks, collapsible groups, theme and density switch at the bottom; pill-style segmented tabs; floating command bar with overflow; filter chips; role cards that double as filters; top-labelled filled fields; currency chip beside the amount; sticky floating save bar on the form.

**Why better.** Than Dynamics 365: far lighter to look at, so long sessions tire less, while keeping the same list, details, tabs and command structure. Than Odoo: fewer borders and boxes, one accent, aligned numbers and a details card rather than a full-page switch. Best fit for owner-managers and sales teams who open the system many times a day and want it to feel fast and uncluttered; the price is slightly fewer rows per screen than A.
