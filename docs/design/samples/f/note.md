# Direction F: "Best of all"

The synthesis: A's density and speed, B's lightness, C's Arabic typography and calm, plus four working touches none of them had. A light warm sidebar with collapsible groups, a recent-records list and both wordmarks; a quiet top bar with breadcrumb, density, theme and language; content on soft white cards with almost no lines; Compact rows by default.

**Palette.** Warm paper ground #FAF9F7 and a barely warm sidebar #F4F2EE (from C's sand, toned right down), white cards, filled fields #F4F3F0 instead of boxes (B). Ink #1D1B26 for text; brand violet #6A00E8 (light) / #9B5CFF (dark) only for the one primary action, selection, focus and the timeline's progress. Status tones are quiet tinted pills (green, amber, red, blue), AA on their tints; the next-best-action hint uses a soft amber note so it is seen without alarming. Dark is a calm plum-grey (#131219 ground, #1A1922 cards), not neon. High contrast is black with white rules, a yellow focus and selection edge, and borders restored on every card and field through tokens.

**Type.** English: Inter (variable, SIL OFL), tabular figures on every number, decimals of headline figures in a lighter tone. Arabic: Noto Kufi Arabic for the interface and Noto Naskh Arabic for titles and section heads (C's pairing, SIL OFL); Arabic is one step larger, running text at 1.85 leading, titles at 1.5, dense one-line cells at 1.5 so Compact rows still hold. The stack is one token per role, so Arabic text never falls to a Latin face; codes and amounts stay Latin-digit and left-to-right inside Arabic lines.

**Spacing and shape.** 4 px grid. Compact (default): 32 px rows, 30 px controls, 8 px cell padding. Comfortable: 42 px rows, 36 px controls. Radius 4 to 14 px; layered low shadows instead of borders; dashed outline only for the empty state and add-filter chip.

**Components.** Sidebar with "Jump to" (Ctrl K), open group with an accent tick, counts (14 overdue invoices) and recent records. Contacts: saved-view tabs with counts (All, My customers, Overdue 14, Credit stop 3, Free zone, Save view); search, filter chips, Group by and Sort pickers; a grid grouped by status with sticky group headers carrying count and AED base-currency subtotals of overdue and balance (collapsible); credit-used micro-bars; a details pane with a next-best-action note ("3 invoices overdue, 18,420.00 AED: Send statement"), key figures, an inline order timeline (Quoted, Ordered, Delivered, Invoiced, Paid) and details. Form: record header with key facts, tabs, the same next-best-action banner, three-column top-labelled sections, money fields with a currency chip and base-currency line (three decimals for KWD, BHD, OMR), dd/mm/yyyy dates with picker, inline error on Accounts email, a vertical order timeline, credit with trend, record audit, and a floating save bar with change and error counts. Dashboard: actions first (overdue to chase, approvals, stock to reorder, credit breached) each with its own action and key, then five key figures with 12-month bars, then activity and a guided empty state. Settings: users grid, role list with the open role's permission matrix and members, access changes and sign-in policy. Sign-in: one centred card, English wordmark with the Arabic one under it.

**From A, B and C.** A: Compact rows, a full command structure, a grid that keeps the list in view beside the record, keyboard hints. B: the light sidebar, filled fields, few lines, cards with soft depth, the floating save bar. C: Kufi and Naskh Arabic with real leading, a warm calm ground, the premium feel without the lower density.

**Why better.** Than A, B and C: it keeps A's rows per screen while being as easy on the eye as B and as good in Arabic as C, and it adds what the others only list: views people save, totals that group themselves, the order's progress at a glance and the next step suggested on the record. Than Dynamics 365: no grey slabs or ribbon clutter, real right-to-left with a designed Arabic face, currency and correct minor digits beside every amount, and subtotals in base currency without building a report. Than Odoo: the list and the record side by side instead of a page switch, grouped subtotals that stay visible while scrolling, a dashboard that says what to do today, and one token set for light, dark and high contrast in both languages.
