# Direction C: "Majlis"

Calm and premium, with the best Arabic. A floating ink navigation panel, a header band with a faint eight-point star lattice (the only UAE motif: pattern and colour, no pictures), ivory sheets with hairline gold rules, serif titles and dotted row separators.

**Palette.** Sand and ivory ground (#F4EFE6, #FFFDFA), deep ink-violet navigation (#17112E), brand violet #6A00E8 for the main action, a desert gold (#A8761C light, #DDB45F dark) for hairlines, currency codes, icons and the selection edge, Gulf-sea green for positive status. The dark theme moves to plum-night with warm gold. High contrast drops the pattern and turns gold into yellow.

**Type.** English UI: Manrope (variable, SIL OFL); English display titles: Fraunces (variable, SIL OFL). Arabic UI: Noto Kufi Arabic (SIL OFL), geometric and a close partner of Manrope; Arabic display titles and section heads: Noto Naskh Arabic (SIL OFL), calligraphic and warm. In Arabic the titles are 42 px with 1.5 leading, body 14 px with 1.85 leading, section heads in Naskh. Tabular figures; currency codes in gold small caps beside every amount.

**Spacing and shape.** Generous: Comfortable default (54 px rows, 42 px controls), Compact 36 px. Radius 8 to 26 px, pill buttons and chips. Soft long shadows. Long names wrap to two lines in Comfortable instead of being cut.

**Components.** Collapsible navigation with gold dot on the open page; header band with key facts separated by gold hairlines; ribbon command bar with overflow; split sheet (grid and a details panel with its own pattern header); tabbed form with sectioned soft cards and a credit aside; master list of roles beside the users grid; sign-in with a centred card over the lattice, English wordmark with the Arabic one under it.

**Why better.** Than Dynamics 365: warmer and more distinctive, with Arabic typography that is designed rather than substituted (Naskh and Kufi pairing, correct leading, right-to-left layout of every control). Than Odoo: the premium feel clients expect of a finance system for the Gulf, without losing the list, details and form structure. Best fit for companies where the owner shows the system to partners and banks, and where Arabic is the working language; the cost is lower density than A.
