# p10 — Document numbering

- Number sequences per tenant, company, branch and document type; patterns with prefix,
  suffix, padding, year or month tokens; reset per period; gapless option that is safe under
  concurrency; preview of the next number.
- Contract service later modules call to take a number inside their transaction.
- Screens and API to configure; audit of changes.

Compared against Odoo: configure a sequence with a yearly reset and preview the next number.
