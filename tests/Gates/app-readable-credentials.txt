# Columns whose name says they hold a secret (password, secret, hash, credential, token) that the
# application role may nevertheless read. Reviewed list: `schema.table.column  # reason`.
# The G1 credential gate fails on any other readable secret column, and on stale entries.
identity.sessions.token_hash  # SHA-256 of a 256-bit random session token. It cannot be turned back into the token, and HTTP requests authenticate only with the token itself, so reading it signs nobody in. Sign-out and session lists match sessions by id, not by this column.
tenancy.companies.logo_hash  # SHA-256 of the company's logo image, which is not a secret: the logo itself is readable by every user of the company (GET /companies/{id}/logo) and printed on documents. The hash is only the logo's ETag and the audit trail's stand-in for the image bytes. It protects nothing and signs nobody in.
